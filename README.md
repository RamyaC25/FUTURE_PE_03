# FUTURE_PE_03 – SEO Content Strategy for a Local Business

## Task 3 – Prompt Engineering Internship

### Business Information

**Business Name:** Glow Beauty Salon  
**Business Type:** Beauty Salon  
**Location:** Shivamogga, Karnataka

### Project Objective

The objective of this project is to use structured AI prompts to create an SEO-focused content strategy for a local beauty salon.

The project uses a pillar-and-supporting-content approach to create useful, locally relevant content around beauty, hair care, self-care and salon services.

### Target Audience

Women and girls in Shivamogga who are interested in:

- Beauty services
- Hair care
- Grooming
- Self-care
- Bridal makeup
- Salon services

### Content Deliverables

The SEO Content Pack includes:

- 1 Pillar Blog
- 5 Supporting Blogs
- Keyword and search-intent planning
- SEO-friendly H1, H2 and H3 structure
- Local SEO content
- Internal linking suggestions
- Business-focused CTAs
- Prompt logic
- AI workflow
- Content cluster structure
- Internal linking map
- SEO quality checklist
- Key learnings
- Conclusion

### SEO & Keyword Strategy

The content strategy considers:

- Primary keywords
- Related search topics
- Search intent
- Local SEO
- Informational content
- Service-related searches
- Internal linking opportunities

The content was designed to target relevant searches while keeping keywords natural and avoiding keyword stuffing.

### Prompting Approach

The prompts were structured using:

1. Business information
2. Target audience
3. Primary keywords
4. Search intent
5. Content requirements
6. SEO structure
7. Local SEO requirements
8. Internal linking requirements
9. CTA requirements
10. Quality constraints

### Internal Linking

Internal linking suggestions were included to connect the pillar article with relevant supporting articles.

The strategy helps organize the content into a clear topic cluster while providing readers with relevant related information.

### Quality Constraints

The prompts instructed the AI to:

- Keep content useful and readable.
- Use keywords naturally.
- Avoid keyword stuffing.
- Avoid fake claims and unrealistic promises.
- Avoid unsupported statistics.
- Avoid invented reviews and ratings.
- Avoid invented prices and offers.
- Avoid guaranteed results.
- Avoid medical claims.
- Keep local references natural.
- Make the content suitable for a real local business.
